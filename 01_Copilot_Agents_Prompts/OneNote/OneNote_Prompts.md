# 40 Use-Case-Driven Copilot Prompts for OneNote

Replace bracketed placeholders with your organization-specific details and review generated output before use.

**Prompt count: 40**

## 1. Weekly planning

Using the notes on this page, build a weekly plan with top outcomes, appointments, focused-work blocks, dependencies, risks, and a realistic priority order.

## 2. Meeting preparation

Prepare me for [meeting] using these notes. Summarize context, prior decisions, open actions, stakeholder concerns, questions to ask, and desired outcomes.

## 3. Meeting notes cleanup

Organize these raw meeting notes into topics, decisions, actions, owners, deadlines, parking-lot items, and follow-up questions. Do not infer attendees or decisions.

## 4. Action register

Extract all commitments from this notebook section into an action register with action, owner if stated, due date if stated, source page, status, and dependency.

## 5. Project notebook structure

Create a OneNote structure for [project] with sections for charter, stakeholders, plan, meetings, decisions, risks, issues, actions, deliverables, lessons, and archive.

## 6. Research notebook

Organize these research notes into themes, evidence, counterpoints, source references, unanswered questions, and implications for [decision].

## 7. Decision preparation

Turn these notes into a decision brief with decision statement, context, criteria, options, evidence, trade-offs, risks, and recommended next step.

## 8. Brainstorm synthesis

Cluster these ideas into themes, remove duplicates, identify high-potential concepts, note assumptions, and propose experiments to test the top three.

## 9. Customer discovery

Organize customer interview notes into jobs, pains, gains, quotes, unmet needs, objections, and opportunity hypotheses. Keep observations separate from interpretation.

## 10. Stakeholder map

Create a stakeholder map from these notes with interests, influence, impact, current position, engagement needs, and next interaction. Flag missing evidence.

## 11. Risk workshop

Convert workshop notes into a risk list with cause, event, consequence, likelihood placeholder, impact placeholder, controls, owner, mitigation, and trigger.

## 12. Lessons learned

Synthesize retrospective notes into what worked, what did not, root contributors, practices to repeat, changes to try, and owners for improvement actions.

## 13. Learning plan

Create a 12-week learning plan for [skill] using these resources. Include weekly goals, practice tasks, checkpoints, evidence of learning, and reflection prompts.

## 14. Book or course notes

Summarize these learning notes into key concepts, examples, practical applications, questions, and a spaced-review checklist.

## 15. SOP capture

Turn these process notes into a structured draft SOP with prerequisites, ordered steps, controls, exceptions, escalation, and evidence to retain.

## 16. Personal knowledge index

Create an index for this notebook section showing topic, page title, short description, keywords, related pages, and last-review placeholder.

## 17. Daily briefing

Summarize today’s notes into priorities, meetings, deadlines, blockers, people to contact, and items that can be delegated or deferred.

## 18. End-of-day review

Create an end-of-day review from these notes: completed outcomes, unfinished work, decisions, lessons, follow-ups, and tomorrow’s first action.

## 19. 1:1 preparation

Prepare a neutral 1:1 agenda from these notes with progress, support needed, development topics, decisions, commitments, and follow-up. Avoid personality judgments.

## 20. Workshop agenda

Create a facilitated workshop agenda for [objective] with outcomes, participants placeholder, pre-work, timeboxed activities, prompts, materials, breaks, and decision points.

## 21. Workshop synthesis

Organize workshop outputs into shared goals, themes, decisions, disagreements, ideas, actions, owners, and unresolved questions.

## 22. Change impact notes

Structure these change notes by stakeholder, current process, future process, impact, readiness, communication, training, support, and risk.

## 23. Requirements capture

Convert these notes into business requirements with ID, user need, requirement, rationale, priority placeholder, acceptance criteria, dependency, and source.

## 24. User story backlog

Turn these notes into user stories with persona, need, outcome, acceptance criteria, dependencies, assumptions, and open questions.

## 25. Architecture notes

Organize technical notes into context, components, interfaces, data, security, non-functional requirements, decisions, risks, and open design questions.

## 26. Incident timeline

Create a factual incident timeline from these notes with timestamp, event, source, action, owner if stated, impact, and evidence gap.

## 27. Vendor meeting notes

Summarize vendor notes into commitments, deliverables, commercial points, risks, dependencies, questions, and next negotiation actions.

## 28. Board pack notes

Create a board-pack preparation checklist from these notes: required papers, owners, review dates, decisions, risks, evidence, approvals, and distribution controls.

## 29. Executive themes

Synthesize this notebook section into the five themes an executive should know, supporting evidence, implications, decisions, and next steps.

## 30. Idea-to-proposal

Turn this idea notebook into a proposal outline with problem, users, value, solution, assumptions, evidence, pilot, measures, risks, and requested sponsorship.

## 31. Content calendar

Create a content calendar from these ideas with audience, message, format, channel, owner, due date placeholder, review stage, and reuse opportunities.

## 32. Hiring notes organizer

Organize role-planning notes into demand, skills, level, location, timing, sourcing approach, interview panel placeholder, risks, and approval questions.

## 33. Onboarding checklist

Create a role-based onboarding checklist from these notes for day 1, week 1, first 30 days, 60 days, and 90 days, with owners and evidence of completion.

## 34. Policy Q&A notes

Create an employee-facing Q&A from these policy notes. Use plain language, cite the relevant note/page, and flag questions requiring HR or legal confirmation.

## 35. Travel or event plan

Create a structured event plan from these notes with objectives, itinerary, bookings, participants placeholder, budget, dependencies, risks, and contingency actions.

## 36. Innovation backlog

Convert ideas into an innovation backlog with problem, proposed experiment, target user, expected value, effort placeholder, risk, evidence needed, and next test.

## 37. OKR planning notes

Turn strategy notes into draft objectives and measurable key results. Link each result to evidence, owner placeholder, cadence, dependencies, and risks.

## 38. Portfolio notebook

Create a portfolio summary across project pages with objective, status, milestone, benefit, risk, decision, and next action. Cite the source page for each item.

## 39. Knowledge gaps

Review these notes and list contradictions, outdated items, missing sources, unanswered questions, and topics that require subject-matter validation.

## 40. Notebook executive summary

Create a one-page executive summary of this notebook section covering purpose, current state, key evidence, decisions, risks, actions, and unresolved questions.
