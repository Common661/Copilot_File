# 40 Use-Case-Driven Copilot Prompts for Word

Replace bracketed placeholders with your organization-specific details and review generated output before use.

**Prompt count: 40**

## 1. Executive briefing

Using [source files], draft a two-page executive briefing for [audience] with context, key facts, implications, options, recommendation, risks, and next steps. Keep the tone concise and cite the source for each material claim.

## 2. Board paper

Draft a board paper on [decision] with purpose, decision required, background, strategic alignment, financial impact, key risks, alternatives, management recommendation, and proposed resolution.

## 3. Project proposal

Create a project proposal for [initiative] covering problem statement, objectives, scope, deliverables, approach, timeline, governance, resourcing, costs, benefits, risks, and approval required.

## 4. Business case

Build a decision-ready business case for [initiative] using [sources]. Include baseline, options, quantified benefits and costs, assumptions, sensitivity, implementation roadmap, and benefits tracking.

## 5. Policy draft

Draft a policy for [topic] with purpose, scope, principles, roles, mandatory requirements, exceptions, monitoring, consequences, and review cycle. Mark areas requiring legal, HR, security, or compliance validation.

## 6. Standard operating procedure

Turn these process notes into an SOP with purpose, prerequisites, roles, step-by-step procedure, controls, evidence, escalation paths, and version history.

## 7. Client meeting brief

Create a pre-meeting brief for [client/meeting] from [sources]. Include relationship context, objectives, recent developments, stakeholder interests, open items, risks, questions to ask, and desired outcomes.

## 8. Meeting minutes

Convert these notes into formal minutes with attendees as provided, agenda items, discussion summary, decisions, actions, owners, due dates, and unresolved questions. Do not infer attendance.

## 9. Change communication plan

Draft a change communication plan for [program] with stakeholder groups, key messages, channels, cadence, owners, feedback loops, resistance risks, and success measures.

## 10. Leadership announcement

Draft a leadership announcement about [change]. Explain what is changing, why, who is affected, timing, required actions, support available, and FAQs. Use an empathetic and transparent tone.

## 11. Job description

Create a job description for [role] with role purpose, outcomes, responsibilities, required and preferred skills, experience, behavioral competencies, reporting context placeholder, and inclusive language.

## 12. Interview guide

Create a structured interview guide for [role] with competency-based questions, scenario questions, evidence indicators, consistent scoring anchors, and prohibited or bias-prone topics to avoid.

## 13. Onboarding guide

Draft a 30-60-90 day onboarding guide for [role] with learning goals, stakeholders, systems, compliance tasks, early deliverables, checkpoints, and success indicators.

## 14. Training manual

Create a training manual for [process/tool] with learning objectives, prerequisites, modules, examples, practice exercises, knowledge checks, troubleshooting, and job aids.

## 15. Service description

Draft a client-facing service description for [service] covering outcomes, scope, exclusions, service levels, responsibilities, dependencies, support model, security, and change control.

## 16. Statement of work

Draft a statement of work from [proposal/notes] with objectives, scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, timeline, RACI, assumptions, dependencies, fees placeholder, change control, and sign-off.

## 17. Risk assessment

Create a risk assessment narrative from [risk register]. Group risks by theme, explain causes and consequences, evaluate controls as documented, and propose actions for owner review.

## 18. Audit response

Draft a factual management response to [audit finding]. Include agreement or clarification, root cause, corrective action, owner placeholder, target date placeholder, evidence, and sustainability measures.

## 19. Incident report

Create a neutral incident report from [records]. Include timeline, observed impact, containment, evidence, root-cause status, corrective actions, lessons learned, and unanswered questions. Avoid unsupported blame.

## 20. Lessons learned report

Synthesize project feedback into a lessons-learned report with what worked, what did not, contributing factors, reusable practices, corrective actions, owners, and applicability to future projects.

## 21. Research synthesis

Synthesize [sources] on [topic] into an evidence-based report. Separate consensus, conflicting evidence, gaps, implications, and recommendations. Include source citations and publication dates.

## 22. RFP response

Draft a response to [RFP section] using approved source material. Map each requirement to a clear response, evidence, differentiators, assumptions, and gaps requiring SME input.

## 23. Vendor evaluation report

Create a vendor evaluation report from the scorecards and notes. Describe criteria, evidence, strengths, concerns, commercial considerations, risks, and recommendation without inventing scores.

## 24. Operating model

Draft a target operating model for [function] covering capabilities, processes, organization, governance, technology, data, controls, service measures, transition steps, and design principles.

## 25. Strategy document

Draft a three-year strategy for [function/business] using [sources]. Include ambition, current state, strategic choices, initiatives, capabilities, investment themes, roadmap, KPIs, risks, and governance.

## 26. Market-entry memo

Prepare a market-entry memo for [market] based on [sources]. Cover demand, customer segments, competition, route to market, regulations, economics, risks, entry options, and recommendation.

## 27. Case study

Turn [project materials] into a client-safe case study with challenge, context, approach, solution, measurable outcomes, lessons, and call to action. Remove confidential details and mark unverified claims.

## 28. Technical design summary

Create an architecture summary from [design documents] for mixed technical and business readers. Explain components, integrations, data flows, security, non-functional requirements, decisions, and open issues.

## 29. Data migration plan

Draft a data migration plan covering scope, data profiling, mapping, cleansing, migration waves, reconciliation, security, cutover, rollback, ownership, and acceptance criteria.

## 30. Test strategy

Create a test strategy for [solution] with scope, test levels, environments, data, entry and exit criteria, traceability, defect management, automation, non-functional testing, and reporting.

## 31. Implementation roadmap

Convert approved recommendations into an implementation roadmap with workstreams, phases, milestones, dependencies, decision gates, owners, resources, risks, and measurable outcomes.

## 32. Benefits realization plan

Draft a benefits realization plan for [program] with benefit definitions, baselines, targets, owners, measurement methods, evidence sources, reporting cadence, dependencies, and corrective-action triggers.

## 33. Executive summary rewrite

Rewrite this document into a one-page executive summary. Preserve facts, emphasize decisions and business impact, remove repetition, explain technical terms, and add a concise next-step section.

## 34. Plain-language rewrite

Rewrite the selected content for a non-specialist audience at [reading level]. Preserve meaning, shorten sentences, define necessary jargon, and provide a glossary for unavoidable terms.

## 35. Regulator-ready rewrite

Rewrite this document in a precise, neutral, regulator-ready style. Separate facts, assumptions, judgments, and forward-looking statements. Flag claims that need evidence or legal review.

## 36. Contract review checklist

Using this contract, create a review checklist of obligations, deliverables, dates, service levels, fees, liabilities, termination rights, data protection, and ambiguous clauses. This is not legal advice; flag items for counsel.

## 37. Knowledge article

Turn the resolved issue in [notes/ticket] into a knowledge article with symptoms, environment, cause, resolution, validation, rollback, prevention, and related references.

## 38. FAQ document

Create an FAQ for [change/product/process] using [sources]. Group questions by audience, answer in plain language, link each answer to evidence, and mark questions that require owner confirmation.

## 39. Decision log

Create a structured decision log from these meeting notes with decision, date, context, options considered, rationale, approver as documented, impacts, dependencies, and review date.

## 40. Document quality review

Review this document for completeness, consistency, unsupported claims, ambiguous wording, duplicated content, accessibility, and tone. Provide prioritized edits and then a revised version.
