# 40 Use-Case-Driven Copilot Prompts for PowerPoint

Replace bracketed placeholders with your organization-specific details and review generated output before use.

**Prompt count: 40**

## 1. Board performance deck

Create a 10-slide board deck from [sources] covering executive summary, KPI performance, financials, customer, operations, people, risk, strategic initiatives, decisions required, and next steps. Use one message per slide and add speaker notes.

## 2. Quarterly business review

Build a QBR deck for [business unit] using [sources]. Show results versus target, drivers, customer outcomes, delivery, pipeline, risks, priorities, and asks. Use charts only where the source data supports them.

## 3. CEO strategy update

Create an executive strategy update with ambition, market context, progress against priorities, strategic choices, investment trade-offs, top risks, and decisions required.

## 4. CFO variance review

Create a finance review deck showing actual versus budget, forecast changes, revenue and cost drivers, cash position, scenario outlook, corrective actions, and approvals required.

## 5. CHRO workforce review

Create a workforce review deck covering headcount, hiring, attrition, skills, succession, engagement, workforce risks, and prioritized people actions. Use aggregated information only.

## 6. Sales pitch

Create a client pitch for [client] using [brief]. Structure it around client priorities, value hypothesis, proposed solution, proof points, delivery approach, commercial next steps, and discussion questions.

## 7. Proposal defense

Build a presentation to defend [proposal] before evaluators. Map the story to evaluation criteria, highlight evidence and differentiators, address likely objections, and include a Q&A appendix.

## 8. Project kickoff

Create a project kickoff deck with objectives, scope, deliverables, timeline, governance, roles, ways of working, risks, dependencies, communication cadence, and immediate next steps.

## 9. Steering committee

Create a steering-committee deck showing overall status, achievements, milestones, budget, risks, issues, decisions, changes, dependencies, and actions. Put decision requests at the start.

## 10. Program roadmap

Turn [roadmap source] into a visual phased roadmap with workstreams, milestones, dependencies, decision gates, benefits, and ownership. Include a slide explaining sequencing logic.

## 11. Transformation story

Create a transformation narrative from current state to target state. Show why change is needed, design principles, future experience, capability shifts, roadmap, benefits, and risks.

## 12. Change town hall

Create a town-hall presentation explaining [change] in clear employee language: why now, what changes, what stays, impact by audience, timeline, support, feedback channels, and FAQs.

## 13. Training deck

Create an instructor-led training deck on [topic] with objectives, concept explanation, demonstrations, worked examples, practice activities, knowledge checks, and takeaways.

## 14. Product launch

Build a product launch deck covering customer problem, target segment, proposition, capabilities, differentiation, pricing approach, go-to-market plan, launch readiness, risks, and success measures.

## 15. Market analysis

Create a market analysis presentation using [sources]. Cover market size as documented, trends, segments, customer needs, competitors, opportunities, uncertainties, and recommended strategic questions.

## 16. Competitive comparison

Create a fair competitor comparison based only on [sources]. Compare capabilities, positioning, pricing where available, strengths, limitations, and implications. Mark missing or outdated evidence.

## 17. Investment committee

Create an investment committee deck with thesis, market, economics, management, diligence findings, value-creation plan, downside case, risks, valuation assumptions, and decision required.

## 18. M&A integration

Create an integration planning deck showing deal rationale, Day 1 priorities, workstreams, governance, synergies, people and culture, technology, risks, milestones, and first 100 days.

## 19. Risk committee

Create a risk committee deck with risk profile, movement since prior review, threshold breaches, emerging risks, control status, scenarios, remediation, and decisions required.

## 20. Cybersecurity briefing

Create a leadership cybersecurity briefing using [sources]. Cover threat context, incidents, control posture, vulnerabilities, regulatory concerns, investment priorities, and decisions. Avoid exposing sensitive technical details unnecessarily.

## 21. IT architecture review

Create an architecture review deck with business requirements, current state, proposed architecture, integrations, data flows, security, non-functional requirements, options, decisions, and migration roadmap.

## 22. Cloud migration update

Create a cloud migration status deck with wave progress, applications, dependencies, cost, risk, security, operational readiness, lessons learned, and next-wave decisions.

## 23. Operational excellence

Create an operations improvement deck showing baseline performance, bottlenecks, root causes, improvement ideas, prioritized initiatives, expected impact, implementation plan, and control measures.

## 24. Supply chain review

Build a supply-chain review deck covering demand, inventory, supplier performance, logistics, service levels, disruptions, scenarios, mitigations, and executive decisions.

## 25. Customer experience review

Create a customer-experience deck using survey and service data. Show journey pain points, evidence, priority moments, root causes, initiatives, expected outcomes, and measurement plan.

## 26. ESG update

Create an ESG progress deck from approved data. Show commitments, performance, initiatives, gaps, risks, assurance status, and next actions. Clearly label estimates and incomplete data.

## 27. Annual planning

Create an annual planning deck with prior-year lessons, assumptions, objectives, initiatives, resource needs, budget, dependencies, risks, KPIs, and leadership choices.

## 28. Scenario planning

Create a scenario-planning deck with three plausible scenarios based on [sources], key drivers, signposts, financial or operational implications, no-regret actions, and trigger-based responses.

## 29. Demo storyline

Create a live-demo deck for [solution] with audience problem, demo persona, setup, step-by-step scenario, expected result, business value, fallback screenshots placeholder, and closing questions.

## 30. Agent demo

Create a Copilot agent demo deck showing user problem, data sources, starter prompts, live interaction, responsible-AI controls, expected outputs, limitations, adoption plan, and success measures.

## 31. Board meeting agent

Create a demo deck for a Board Meeting Agent that prepares agenda briefs, retrieves approved board materials, tracks decisions, and follows up actions. Include sample prompts, permission boundaries, human review, and demo script.

## 32. HR onboarding agent

Create an onboarding-agent demo deck covering employee questions, policy grounding, role-based checklists, learning resources, escalation to HR, privacy safeguards, sample prompts, and adoption measures.

## 33. Finance close agent

Create a finance-close agent demo deck covering checklist tracking, variance commentary, evidence retrieval, issue escalation, status summaries, sample prompts, controls, and approval boundaries.

## 34. Manager day in the life

Create a day-in-the-life deck showing how a manager can prepare meetings, summarize updates, coach work products, track commitments, plan capacity, communicate change, and protect sensitive information.

## 35. Executive one-page story

Condense this presentation into five slides: situation, key evidence, implications, recommendation, and decision. Preserve source-backed facts and move supporting detail to an appendix.

## 36. Presentation redesign brief

Review this deck and propose a redesign plan. Identify slides with unclear messages, excessive text, weak evidence, inconsistent terminology, or accessibility issues. Provide revised titles and layout guidance.

## 37. Speaker notes

Create concise speaker notes for every slide: objective, key message, evidence to emphasize, transition, likely question, and response grounded in the deck.

## 38. Q&A appendix

Generate a Q&A appendix from this deck with likely executive questions, concise evidence-based answers, source slide references, and a list of questions that need subject-matter confirmation.

## 39. Data-to-story

Turn the charts and tables in [source] into a presentation narrative. For each slide, state the insight, evidence, implication, and action. Do not add unsupported numbers.

## 40. Presentation quality review

Review this deck for narrative flow, factual consistency, readability, accessibility, visual hierarchy, duplicated content, and executive relevance. Recommend prioritized slide-level changes.
